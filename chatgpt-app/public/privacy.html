Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 September 2026

Music Release Manager processes only the release details a user chooses to provide in a tool request, such as artist name, release title, release date, current stage, and checklist answers.

The service does not require an account, does not access Spotify or social-media accounts, and does not store tool inputs or results in a database. Runtime infrastructure may produce short-lived operational logs needed for reliability and security; these logs are not used for advertising or sold to third parties.

Do not enter passwords, payment information, government identifiers, private listening credentials, or other unnecessary sensitive information.
